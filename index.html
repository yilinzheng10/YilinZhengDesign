I am a forward deployed computational designer, design engineer, and researcher based in .
My works explore the intersection of emerging technologies, spatial data, product building, and digital-physical systems.

I work to challenge how we interact with physical spaces and our perception of "constructed" and "in-situ" environment. I believe that the essence of technology is by no means purely technological, and should be, about the system it embodies.
